# SSC CGL Maths Practice - Average (100 Authentic PYQs)

A mobile-friendly, interactive Quantitative Aptitude exam practice platform dedicated to **Average (औसत)**, featuring authentic **SSC CGL Tier-1 and Tier-2 Previous Year Questions (Medium & Hard levels)**.

## Live Demo
Access the live practice test directly on GitHub Pages:

## What's Included:
- **100 Authentic SSC CGL PYQs** categorized into **5 Curated Sets** of **20 questions each**:
  - 📘 **Set 1 (Medium):** Replacements, Inclusions, Exclusions & Consecutive Series
  - 📘 **Set 2 (Medium):** Batting & Bowling Averages, Score Correction & Temperature Variations
  - 🔥 **Set 3 (Hard / Tier-2):** Weighted Ratios, Percentage Differentials & Overlapping Middle Numbers
  - 🔥 **Set 4 (Hard / Tier-2):** Highest-Lowest Extremes, Reversals & Harmonic Speeds
  - 🔥 **Set 5 (Hard / Tier-2):** Complex Multi-Group Shifts, Missing Observations & Advanced Algebra
- **Difficulty Tabs:**
  - `All Sets (5 Sets • 100 Qs)`
  - `Medium Level (2 Sets • 40 Qs)`
  - `Hard / Tier-2 Level (3 Sets • 60 Qs)`
- **Detailed Step-by-Step Solutions:**
  - 📌 **Standard Algebraic & Formula Method**
  - ⚡ **Net Deviation Shortcut ($\Sigma \Delta$)** for lightning fast 20–30 second solving!
- **Exam Testing Features:**
  - Question Palette (1–20) with live question jump.
  - Test timer & sticky navigation.
  - Scorecard with score, accuracy %, correct/incorrect/skipped counts, and time taken.
  - Interactive solution review with filter tabs (`All`, `Incorrect`, `Correct`, `Skipped`).
  - Score persistence in `localStorage`.
