# SSC CGL GK Practice Test (100 Authentic PYQs)

A mobile-friendly, interactive General Knowledge exam practice platform tailored for SSC CGL aspirants.

## Features
- **5 Curated Practice Sets** with **20 questions each** (100 Authentic SSC CGL PYQs).
- **Balanced Topic Distribution**:
  - 📚 Books & Authors
  - 🌲 National Parks of India
  - 🌊 Major Lakes of India
  - 📅 Important Indian & International Days
- **Real Exam Experience**:
  - Test timer & live progress bar.
  - Interactive Question Palette (1 to 20) with quick jump capability.
  - Answers hidden during test to simulate actual exam pressure.
- **Scorecard & Detailed Solutions**:
  - Instant score calculation with accuracy percentage and time taken.
  - Answer Key with interactive filter tabs (*All*, *Incorrect*, *Correct*, *Skipped*).
  - High-yield **SSC Revision Note** under every question for quick revision.
  - Local score persistence in `localStorage`.
- **Ultra Responsive & Mobile-First**:
  - Optimized for touch screens, smartphones, tablets, and desktop.
